arkHive privacy policy

Last updated 12 October 2026.

arkHive is a personal tool used only by its owner, on his own accounts. It has no other users.

What it reads

With the owner's permission, granted on Google's own consent screen, arkHive reads his Gmail messages and Google Drive files (read-only). It never sends, changes or deletes mail or files.

Where the data goes

Everything it reads is stored on the owner's own devices: his computer and a home server with an encrypted drive. It is not sold, shared, or used for advertising. It is not used to train AI models.

Google user data

arkHive's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and from arkHive's own Connections page.
